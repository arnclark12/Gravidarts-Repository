Gravidarts

- A new spin on darts

Play with gravity! Aim your shots to maximize leverage and control the board’s position. One miss, and your opponent may shift the outcome in their favor. Gravidarts combines precision, strategy, and risk in a battle for balance, where any shot can tip the scales.
To understand how it works, click the interactive demo below!

Classic

  • Opponents take turns throwing
  • Aim your darts so the board settles on your half
  • Score the points indicated in the scoring zone
  • Play to 21
  • Last to score shoots first on the next round

Arena

  • Opponents take turns throwing 3 darts at a time
  • Aim your darts so the board settles on your half
  • Score 2 points for settling the board on your half
  • Score 1 point if your dart is in your sweet spot
  • Score 3 points if your dart is in your center zone
  • Play to 21
  • Last to score shoots first on the next round

Arena - 3 Player

  • Opponents take turns throwing 3 darts at a time
  • Aim your darts so the board settles on your third
  • Score 2 points for settling the board on your third
  • Score 1 point if your dart is in your sweet spot
  • Score 3 points if your dart is in your center zone
  • Play to 21
  • Last to score shoots first on the next round

    • Co-op

  • Players take turns throwing 1 dart at a time
  • Aim your darts so the board settles at each colored section
  • Start with the white section and try to make it to the gold section
